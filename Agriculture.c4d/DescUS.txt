Fishing, wheat, apple orchard and smoking — a homestead cycle pack.

Dependencies: the FishTrap (AGFT) spawns a Western MeatBait (MBAT) as its
attract signal, so Western.c4d must be loaded for the trap to function.
The FishingRod (AGFR) requires a crew type that exposes the ThrowFishingPole
action (e.g. the Western Trapper). All other chains are self-contained.
